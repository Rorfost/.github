# Rorfost Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Rorfost to be welcoming, safe, and fair for everyone.

We are committed to an environment that respects the dignity, rights, and contributions of every individual, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status.

Everyone who follows this Code of Conduct can take part in good faith.

## Expected behavior

Please:

1. Respect the purpose of the community, its activities, and its ways of gathering.
2. Be kind and honest with others.
3. Respect different views and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept helpful feedback.
6. Work to repair harm when it occurs.
7. Act in ways that support the well-being of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not allowed. Doing, threatening, or encouraging it breaks this Code of Conduct.

1. **Harassment:** Violating clearly expressed boundaries or continuing unnecessary personal attention after a request to stop.
2. **Character attacks:** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments directed at a community member or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination:** Characterizing anyone's personality or behavior on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualization:** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered inappropriately intimate in the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Violating confidentiality:** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or private information without permission.
6. **Endangerment:** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm toward any person or group.
7. Other behavior that threatens the well-being of the community.

### Other Restrictions

1. **Misleading identity:** Impersonating someone or pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions.
2. **Failing to credit sources:** Not properly crediting sources of contributed content.
3. **Promotional materials:** Sharing marketing or commercial content outside the norms of the community.
4. **Irresponsible communication:** Presenting content that includes, links to, or describes restricted behaviors without appropriate care.

## Reporting a problem

Not every disagreement breaks this Code of Conduct. If you think there is a violation, email [rajpatel7807@gmail.com](mailto:rajpatel7807@gmail.com).

Reports will be handled with care and investigated as appropriate. The maintainer will prioritize safety and confidentiality, and will keep enforcement actions as transparent as practical for the people involved.

## What may happen

When a report finds that this Code of Conduct has been violated, the following actions may be used according to the impact on the people involved and the community. More serious violations may skip earlier steps.

1. **Warning:** A private, written warning. Possible repair may include an apology, acknowledgement of responsibility, or clarification of expectations.
2. **Temporarily limited activities:** A time-limited cooldown that may be limited to particular communication channels or interactions.
3. **Temporary suspension:** A temporary suspension with conditions for return.
4. **Permanent ban:** Removal of access to community spaces, tools, and communication channels when other remedies are insufficient or the violation is severe.

This guidance does not limit the maintainer's judgment in protecting the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Rorfost community spaces and when an individual officially represents Rorfost in public or other spaces. Examples include using an official email address, posting through an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.
